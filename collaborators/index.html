What a collaborator is

Dedicated to the truth

You do not have to believe any of it. You have to care whether it is true. A name on this page does not mean that person agrees with us or endorses us. It means they are in the conversation and helping to test the ideas, whether they turn out right or wrong.

Not for money, not for fame

Neither money nor fame drives this. Everything we make is free forever, with no ads, and any money that ever comes in goes back into the work.

Skeptics wanted

We want open conversations, in writing and in person, with people who think we are wrong and can say why. The place for group talk is our Discord server.

Where help is needed

All advice is welcome. The greatest need is in machine learning, mathematics, and physics. Here are the problems, stated as plainly as we can. We have taken them as far as we can on our own.

Engineering

Simulate the body, unit by unit

A physics simulation of the whole body at the finest grain computing allows. Ideally we would program the body atom by atom; we concede it will probably have to be done down to the cell. Each unit has a tissue type, a 3D shape, a surface where it can join or let go of its neighbors, and the properties it needs to pass on force, pressure, and heat. We start with a master unit that can become any type, the way a stem cell can, fill the shape of a human with them, then use imaging to turn each one into the tissue found at that place.

Mathematics and engineering

Define the ideal biotensegrity

We hold that there is an ideal arrangement of bones and connective tissue (often called fascia). Exactly what it is has to be worked out. Should the bones of the spine stack straight up and down, or not, and why?

Machine learning

Gather the data and give it structure

The properties of every tissue, healthy and not, how tissues act on each other, and what happens to their chemistry when the physics changes. We have begun collecting it, and we need people who know how to gather it properly and build with it.

Engineering

Put the body in the world

A bank of things in the environment suspected of causing disease, each described by its physics, such as radio waves and microwaves. Then place the simulated body in a setting, such as a home beside power lines, and follow what changes downstream.

Mathematics and physics

Finish the mathematics and physics

Our account runs from the first distance toward the known laws, with each open step marked. We want mathematicians and physicists to close them, or to show where they cannot be closed. See hmrphysics.life and hmrmath.life.

Machine learning

Train an HMR guide

An assistant that knows our model thoroughly, like a friend, a family member, a doctor, and an expert in one, that anyone can talk to. We are testing it ourselves first. Doing it properly means training and evaluating a language model of its own, and that needs someone in computer science who knows how.

Machine learning

Simulate a society's values

Model the values that guide a country, then many. Then simulate what happens, decision by decision, if one country adopts our values, or the whole world does. Our hypothesis: if values can be modeled well, ours will come out as the best for any society, and a model that captures them could guide a very capable AI agent.

Clinical research

Human trials

The claims about the human biotensegrity need clinical trials in people, starting with a principal investigator: a qualified clinical researcher who can lead the study and take it through review. We have architected the full first trial, from the hypotheses to the budget and the ethics. Ask us for it.
