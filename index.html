The mission: elevate humanity

Our purpose is to elevate humanity by creating websites that have free solutions to humanity's problems, and that will always be 100% free.

You will never be asked to pay, and you will never see an ad. The flagships and the sites under Done carry that promise for life. The names under Future are free for at least their first year.

How so few people can run all of this

There are no investors and no ads, and nothing here depends on your money. AI now does the work that used to take a team.

We are a very, very small team. We use the highest paid models of Claude, GPT, Grok, and Gemini every day, and any feedback you give is fed through a council of these four to build the sites out.

To everyone who is afraid of AI

The people who are not afraid of AI are the ones out there pushing it to its limits. The ones who are afraid of AI are probably the ones who will use it the most responsibly, and we want to put it in your hands. We are here to help humanity use AI as a force for good. But it really depends on us whether it is.

Safety and responsibility

While we value innovation, we also value not breaking things. All standard security protocols are in place: every page is served over a secure connection, forms send only what you choose to type, and your data is never sold.

If this resonates with you, engage with us here.

What all our sites have that you won't see elsewhere

Every site in the family has the same buttons along the bottom, true to our values: they let you give us signal, express yourself, and enjoy yourself. Learn them on one site, and you know them on all of them. The first time you press each one here, a short note tells you what it is and why it's here.

  • ELV8, the round rainbow button in the middle, opens your dashboard: quick actions, Funsies, a studio for colors, type and layout, and ways to improve the site. To design for perfection is to empower users with customization: engineers build a competent framework, and you change your interface easily.
  • The four arrows around it move you around the page. Up and down jump to the top or the bottom. Left and right step through the sections. Hold one to keep going.
  • The mic, bottom right, is how you give us signal. Press record and talk. Every message is read.
  • React lets you express yourself: pick a feeling and point at the exact spot on the page that caused it.
  • Funsies, inside the dashboard, is a button for joy: fun for us to make, and for you to play.

You can also select any text to highlight it, then share it, send feedback, or email it to yourself. Would you like buttons like these on every website you visit? Tell us with the mic.
