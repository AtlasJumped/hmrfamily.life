    Talk to us any time

    We built many ways for you to tell us what you think. If you like, make this your daily habit: come here and press Speak here whenever you feel like it.

    The round and pill buttons along the bottom of every one of our pages each do one thing.

    • Design lets you change how the page looks and feels, and tell us what you chose.
    • Feedback is the big favor: type or talk and tell us what you think.
    • Back takes you to the page you came from.
    • Funsies opens our toys and games, or lets you wreck the page for fun.
    • Speak here opens a microphone. Say anything you want. Your words appear as text, you can fix them, and one press sends them to us. We want your input.
    • Forward takes you ahead again after you went back.
    • I'm lost tells us where you got stuck, so we can fix it.
    • React lets you pick a feeling, and point at the spot on the page that caused it.

    What we are building next

    We are making a list of things the world needs that are not built yet, or not built for free. For each one we will build a free version. Where someone else already makes a product, paid or not, we will link to it and give it an honest rating against our standard.
