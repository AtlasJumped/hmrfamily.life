The HMR family of websites

We build free tools and ideas, together, with AI. Here is what we stand for.

  • Always free, forever. It is not about money. Nothing we make will ever ask you to pay.
  • Elevating humanity. If we all lift each other, it comes back to us.
  • Open source. We build in the open, as a community of people working with AI.
  • Creating joy. We want what we make to be fun to use.

What every one of our sites has

Every site we build has the same row of buttons along the bottom. You will not find this anywhere else. True to our values, we want everyone who visits to be able to send us a signal, express themselves, and enjoy themselves.

  • Back and Forward, far left and far right, work like your browser's buttons. They are there so you never have to reach for the top of the screen.
  • Design, bottom left, lets you change how the page looks and feels: color, layout, fonts, motion, even your mouse pointer. Pick a style (professional, retro, fantasy, and more), step through the options, and send us what you like. People like different things. We believe the way to design for perfection is to give you the controls. We build a solid starting point, and you shape it.
  • Funsies, in the middle, is a button for joy. Toys, games, and ways to wreck the page for fun. If every website had a fun button at the bottom, you would always know where to go when you need a smile.
  • Speak here, next to Funsies, opens a microphone. Say anything. Your words appear as text, you can fix them, and one press sends them to us.
  • React, bottom right, lets you pick a feeling and point at the exact spot on the page that caused it.

You can also select any text on any of our pages to highlight it, share it, or send us a note about it.

The HMR experiment

Beyond the family, we own more than 130 more web addresses. Each one is an idea, and each has a simple starting site with the same buttons along the bottom.

Here is our plan, in the open. We made a significant investment in these names. We will never ask anyone for money. Each site will do one of three things: sit quietly, get better because people like you give it ideas and feedback, or attract someone who wants to build it, buy it, or support it. If one becomes a real business, or if offers over the next year return more than we spent, the experiment worked. If you want to offer a donation or buy something we make, you are welcome to, and we will listen. We will not ask.

One person owns all of these, working with AI, with no team and no further spending planned. Web addresses are cheap right now. If you have been thinking about building your own sites, this is a good time.

What we are building next

We are making a list of things the world needs that are not built yet, or not built for free. For each one we will build a free version. Where someone else already makes a product, paid or not, we will link to it and give it an honest rating against our standard.

Safety and responsibility

We value new ideas, and we also value not breaking things. Standard security protocols are in place on every site: every page is served over a secure connection, forms send only what you choose to type, and we do not sell your data.
