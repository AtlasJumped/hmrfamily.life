Free forever. Ad-free forever. You will never be asked to pay, and you will never see an ad. The flagships and the sites under Done carry that promise for life. The names under Future are free for at least their first year.

I have a stable remote 9-to-5 job, and its pay covers every server, domain and subscription this family runs on. The payment systems are already set up. There are no investors and no ads, and nothing here depends on your money.

On the dangers of AI

Many people worry that AI is dangerous. I share that concern. My answer is to put all my energy into being a force for good, and to show that AI in the hands of good people does far more good than harm.

Safety and responsibility

While we value innovation, we also value not breaking things. All standard security protocols are in place: every page is served over a secure connection, forms send only what you choose to type, and your data is never sold.

What the buttons along the bottom do

Every HMR family site has the same bar along the bottom, so once you learn it on one site, you know it on all of them.

  • Back and Forward, far left and far right, work like your browser's buttons.
  • Funsies is a button for joy: toys, games, and ways to wreck the page for fun.
  • HMR, in the middle, opens your dashboard: quick actions, a studio for colors, type and layout (Design lives here; it rebuilds the page in a new style you can rate), and ways to improve the site.
  • Speak here, the microphone, opens a small recorder. Press record and talk.
  • React lets you pick a feeling and point at the exact spot on the page that caused it.

You can also select any text to highlight it, then share it, send feedback, or email it to yourself.
