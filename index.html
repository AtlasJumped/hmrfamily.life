The HMR family of websites

We build free tools and ideas, together, with AI. Here is what we stand for.

  • Always free, forever. It is not about money. Nothing we make will ever ask you to pay.
  • Elevating humanity. If we all lift each other, it comes back to us.
  • Open source. We build in the open, as a community of people working with AI.
  • Creating joy. We want what we make to be fun to use.

Safety and responsibility

We value new ideas, and we also value not breaking things. Standard security protocols are in place on every site: every page is served over a secure connection, forms send only what you choose to type, and we do not sell your data.

What every one of our sites has

Every site we build has the same row of buttons along the bottom. True to our values, we want everyone who visits to be able to send us a signal, express themselves, and enjoy themselves.

  • Back and Forward, far left and far right, work like your browser's buttons. They are there so you never have to reach for the top of the screen.
  • Design, bottom left, rebuilds the page from its words in a new style. Shuffle through dozens of designs, go back to one you liked, rate each from hate to love, or switch to text only to proofread. People like different things. We believe the way to design for perfection is to give you the controls. We build a solid starting point, and you shape it.
  • Funsies, in the middle, is a button for joy. Toys, games, and ways to wreck the page for fun. If every website had a fun button at the bottom, you would always know where to go when you need a smile.
  • Speak here, next to Funsies, opens a box where you can talk. Press the record button, wait half a second, and speak. Send it with your email or anonymously.
  • React, bottom right, lets you pick a feeling, rate it from hate to love, and point at the exact spot on the page that caused it. Your signal helps us make every site better, free.

You can also select any text on any of our pages to highlight it. Hover the highlight to share it, send us feedback, or email it to yourself.

Our other domain names

Beyond the HMR family of websites, we own more than 130 domain names, all of which we think will increase in value over time. Each one is an empty canvas, waiting to be painted by artificial intelligence and by the new skills you are about to pick up.

We bought them in one day. More than 30 of them cost under $5 for the first year. The cheapest way we know to build a site costs about $25 ($20 for a month of Claude Pro plus under $5 for a domain), and none of it goes to us. We can build sites and teach others to, so we expect these names to gain value within about six months, whether they are sold or used by our community.

Each name is an idea, and each will have a simple starting site with the same buttons along the bottom. If you have been thinking about building your own sites, this is a good time.
